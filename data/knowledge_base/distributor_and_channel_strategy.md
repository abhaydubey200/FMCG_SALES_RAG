# Distributor and Channel Strategy

## Section 1: Channel Overview

Products reach end consumers through five channels: Modern Trade
(supermarket/hypermarket chains), General Trade (independent small-format
retailers, typically served through distributors rather than directly),
E-Commerce (direct and marketplace), Wholesale (bulk/cash-and-carry), and
Convenience (gas stations, small-format urban stores).

## Section 2: Direct vs. Distributor Coverage

Modern Trade and large E-Commerce accounts are served directly by the
sales organization. General Trade and Convenience are served through
regional distributors, since direct coverage of thousands of small-format
outlets is not economically viable at current route-to-market cost
structures. Wholesale is served through a hybrid model — large wholesale
accounts direct, smaller ones through distributors.

## Section 3: Distributor Performance Standards

Distributors are evaluated quarterly on: sell-through rate (their sales
to retailers, not just their purchases from us), outlet coverage
(percentage of the region's eligible outlets actively stocking our
products), on-time payment rate, and stock rotation compliance
(first-expiry-first-out handling, critical for categories with shorter
shelf life like Beverages and Packaged Foods).

## Section 4: Channel Investment Priority

Modern Trade and E-Commerce receive the highest per-unit marketing
investment given better sell-through visibility and more effective
promotional mechanics (in-store display, digital placement). General
Trade investment is weighted toward distributor incentives and route
-to-market efficiency rather than consumer-facing promotion, since
individual General Trade outlets are too numerous and fragmented for
outlet-level promotional investment to be cost-effective.

## Section 5: New Region Entry

When entering a new region, Modern Trade and E-Commerce listings should
be secured first to establish brand visibility and price reference points
before General Trade distributor onboarding begins — General Trade
retailers are more willing to stock a brand that already has visible
Modern Trade presence, since it reduces their perceived risk on an
unfamiliar product.
