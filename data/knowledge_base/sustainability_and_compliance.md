# Sustainability and Compliance Notes

## Section 1: Packaging Recyclability Targets

By end of fiscal year, 80% of primary packaging by weight should be
recyclable or reusable under standard municipal recycling programs in our
top 10 markets. Beverages and Home Care categories are currently ahead of
this target; Snacks (multi-layer flexible film) is the category furthest
from target and the primary focus of packaging R&D investment.

## Section 2: Supplier Code of Conduct

All raw material and packaging suppliers must attest annually to
compliance with the Supplier Code of Conduct, covering labor standards,
environmental compliance, and conflict-minerals sourcing where
applicable. Non-compliance identified during an audit triggers a 90-day
remediation window before sourcing is paused for that supplier.

## Section 3: Carbon Footprint Reporting

Scope 1 and Scope 2 emissions are reported quarterly at the manufacturing
site level. Scope 3 (supply chain and logistics) is reported annually
given the longer data-collection cycle required from third-party carriers
and suppliers. Distribution-related Scope 3 emissions are the largest
single category, ahead of raw material sourcing, primarily due to
General Trade's more fragmented, less route-optimized delivery pattern
compared to Modern Trade's centralized distribution centers.

## Section 4: Regulatory Compliance Calendar

Formulation and labeling compliance requirements vary by market and are
tracked on a rolling regulatory calendar maintained jointly by Legal and
R&D. A formulation change required in one market should always be
checked against other markets' requirements before implementation, since
a change made solely for one market's compliance can occasionally create
a compliance gap in a different market with different rules.
