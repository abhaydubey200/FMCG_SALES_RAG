# Pricing and Margin Policy

## Section 1: Price Structure

FMCG pricing operates on three levels: List Price (the undiscounted
manufacturer price), Net Price (list price minus standard trade terms),
and Recommended Selling Price / RSP (the suggested retail shelf price,
which the manufacturer does not control but forecasts against for volume
planning).

## Section 2: Promotional Discount Ceiling

Promotional discounts of any kind — off-invoice, bill-back, or
combined — must not exceed **15%** of list price for any single
promotional event, measured on the total value of the promoted order.

> Note: Trade Promotion Policy Section 2 states a lower figure (12%) as
> the standard off-invoice discount ceiling specifically. This Pricing
> and Margin Policy states a higher figure (15%) as the combined ceiling
> across all discount types on a single promotional event. These are not
> contradictory once read carefully — 12% is the default limit for the
> single most common discount type (off-invoice), while 15% is the hard
> ceiling when off-invoice is combined with any other trade spend on the
> same event — but the two numbers should never be quoted interchangeably
> as "the trade discount limit," since doing so misstates one or the
> other depending on which type of promotion is being discussed.

## Section 3: Minimum Margin Requirements

Minimum contribution margin (net price minus unit cost, divided by net
price) by category:

- Beverages: 28% minimum
- Snacks: 32% minimum
- Personal Care: 40% minimum
- Home Care: 35% minimum
- Packaged Foods: 25% minimum

A promotion that would bring a SKU's contribution margin below its
category floor requires Finance sign-off regardless of the discount
percentage itself, since margin floor breaches are tracked independently
of the discount-percentage ceilings in Section 2.

## Section 4: Price Change Governance

List price increases require 8 weeks' advance notice to key accounts.
Price decreases (delistings, clearance, or competitive response) may be
implemented with 2 weeks' notice but require VP Sales approval if the
decrease exceeds 10% of list price and affects more than one region
simultaneously.
